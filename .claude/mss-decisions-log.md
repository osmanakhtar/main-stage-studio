*v6, locked 10 Oct 2026. Full rules: `mss-tone-of-voice.md`; examples: `copy-contexts.md`.*

| Decision | What's locked |
|----------|--------------|
| Character | Plain. Specific. A bit of swagger. The empathy shows in what MSS does, not in long sentences |
