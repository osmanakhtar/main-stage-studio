# Main Stage Studio — Tone of Voice

---

## Brand Character

Quietly confident. The person in the room who doesn't need to prove anything, but when they speak, people listen. Creative enough to earn respect in that world. Structured enough to actually deliver.

## Three Words Clients Use

Creative. Understanding. Capable.

## Personality in Practice

Main Stage Studio sounds like a founder who has done the work — in corporate, in creative, in tech — and is applying all of it here. Not performing expertise. Actually having it.

---

## Principles

**Say what you mean.**
No filler. No throat-clearing. If a sentence doesn't add something, cut it.

**Confidence without ego.**
State things clearly. Don't hedge unnecessarily, but don't oversell either. Let the work and the thinking speak.

**Talk to the human, not the brief.**
Clients are creatives and founders. They care about their work, their audience, their reputation. Lead with their world, not ours.

**Show the thinking, not the jargon.**
"We bring strategic discipline" means nothing. "We figure out what makes you different before we design a single thing" means something.

**Earn trust through substance.**
Every piece of content, every caption, every case study should leave someone thinking — *they actually know what they're doing.*

## Voice in Practice

| Instead of | Say |
|---|---|
| PM rigour applied to creative work | We plan it properly. Then we build it. |
| AI-assisted delivery | We use the best tools available — including AI — to move faster without cutting corners. |
| End-to-end process | From the first conversation to launch day, it's all us. |
| Discovery and positioning | Before anything looks like anything, we figure out what you're actually about. |
| Brand identity | How you look, sound, and feel to the people you want to reach. |

---

## What We Never Sound Like

**Not fluffy** — no "we're passionate about storytelling" without substance behind it.

**Not arrogant** — no "most agencies do X wrong, we do it right."

**Not unsure** — no excessive hedging, qualifying, or over-explaining.

**Not corporate** — no passive voice, no buzzword stacking.
