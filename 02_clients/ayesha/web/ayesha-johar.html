About Ayesha

Thirty years
of paying attention
to what the world
forgets to honour.

Ayesha Johar is an acrylic painter based in London, originally from Mauritius. Her practice spans spirituality, nature, travel, love and the urgent politics of human dignity. She has been making work for over thirty years and selling it for the last ten.

Outside the studio she has spent her career working in the community, supporting vulnerable women. That work and this work are not separate.
