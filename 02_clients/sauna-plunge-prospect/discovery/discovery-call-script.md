# Discovery Call Script: Shak and Rajan

**Status:** internal, for Osman's use on the call
**Length:** 60 minutes
**With:** Shak (Shakir Mughal) and Rajan Sethi
**Background and the full question bank:** `discovery-call-prep.md` in this folder

**How to use this:** lines in quotes are suggested wording, not a script to read out. ★ questions must be asked. "If time" questions get dropped first. Each section has a hard stop; when you hit it, use the bridge line and move on. Anything unanswered goes on the follow-up list at the end.

## 1. Open and set the agenda (4 min, 0:00 to 0:04)

> "Thanks both. The aim today is to understand the business, not just the website, because the site has to do the job the business needs. I'll ask a lot of questions. By the end I want to know enough to send you a clear proposal with options."

> "Rough plan: ten minutes on you and the idea, the biggest chunk on the supplier and how an order works, then name, brand and website, and finally budget and timing. We'll finish on the hour."

- Ask for permission to take notes. If you're recording, ask that too.

**Hard stop 0:04.**

---

## 2. Shak, Rajan and the new business (8 min, 0:04 to 0:12)

> "Start me at the beginning. Why saunas and plunge pools, and why now?"

- ★ "How will the two of you split this? Who does what, and who has the final say on things like the name and spend?"
- ★ "The company doesn't exist yet. Who's setting it up, and when? Will it be separate from Pure Earth?"
- "Is this alongside your other work, or is one of you going full-time on it?"
- If time: "What does a good first year look like? Orders a month, revenue, anything else?"

Listen for: whether Nafisa has any role, how much time they really have, how ambitious they are.

**Bridge at 0:12:** "That's really helpful. You've done this before with Pure Earth, so let me ask what you've learned there."

---

## 3. Pure Earth: what they already know (5 min, 0:12 to 0:17)

> "How does a Pure Earth order work today, from someone clicking buy to it landing on their doorstep?"

- ★ "Who's the supplier there, and how do orders reach them?"
- "What's worked well, and what's been a pain?"
- If time: "What's the plan for Pure Earth alongside this, keep it as is, grow it, wind it down?"

Don't critique the store. Note anything they say about Gmail, the theme or the range not growing; it's useful later.

**Bridge at 0:17:** "This new business works quite differently because of the price and size of the products, so let's talk about the supplier."

---

## 4. The supplier and the model (12 min, 0:17 to 0:29)

The most important section. If you only get answers here and in section 5, the call has done its job.

> "Where are you with Outdoor Living 365? What have they told you so far?"

- ★ "Is it dropship, so they deliver straight to your customer? Have they shared terms yet?"
- ★ "Do you know your margin or trade discount, and how you pay them: per order or on account?"
- ★ "Can you set your own prices, or do you have to match theirs?"
- ★ "Would you sell them as SaunaPlunge, or under your own brand?"

Then the big one:

> ★ "They sell the same products on their own site, at the same prices, with finance and installation. Why would someone buy from you rather than straight from them?"

Let the silence run. If they struggle, offer the options: own brand, a region they serve poorly, full install service, bundles and advice, commercial buyers, their own clientele from the practices.

If not already covered, quick-fire (pick 2 or 3):
- "Will the delivery paperwork and installers carry their brand or yours?"
- "Will they agree not to market to your customers?"
- "Could other resellers sell the same range in your area?"
- "Who handles damage, returns and warranty claims?"

> "Since you're still negotiating, I'll send you a short list of points worth getting in writing with them. It'll protect you whatever you decide on the website."

**Bridge at 0:29:** "Let's walk through one order end to end, because at these prices that's where the website earns its keep."

---

## 5. How a sale and an order will work (8 min, 0:29 to 0:37)

> "Picture someone spending £4,000 on a barrel sauna. Talk me through it from first visit to sitting in it."

- ★ "Do you want people to buy straight online, pay a deposit, or enquire first? Or a mix?"
- ★ "Who checks the garden suits it, access, the base, the electrics, before the order goes to the supplier?"
- "Klarna or PayPal Credit for finance?"
- "Who books the delivery date with the customer? Who installs, and who does the electrics?"
- If time: "Any interest in selling to gyms, physios or holiday lets?"

Plant the money points briefly; don't lecture:
> "Two things worth knowing early. At these prices you'll pass the VAT threshold at about two orders a month. And payment providers sometimes hold back payouts for new stores with long delivery times, so cash flow needs planning. We'll cover both in the proposal."

**Bridge at 0:37:** "Who actually does all this day to day?"

---

## 6. The VA and running it day to day (5 min, 0:37 to 0:42)

> "Will your VA run this one too? Tell me about their role."

- ★ "Where are they based and what hours do they work?"
- ★ "Who picks up the phone? Someone spending £4,000 often wants to talk to a person, and often in the evening."
- "Is any of the process written down, or is it in their head?"
- If time: "Do they use their own Shopify login or yours?"

> "Something I can help with is writing up how the operation runs: order checks, supplier handoff, customer replies. It makes a remote-run store much safer at these values."

**Bridge at 0:42:** "Let's get on to the bit you came to me for: name, brand and the website."

---

## 7. Name, brand, prototype, website, email (8 min, 0:42 to 0:50)

**Name and brand (3 min)**
- ★ "No name yet. Would you like me to help with naming as well as the brand?"
- "Any names on a shortlist, or any you've ruled out?"
- "Should it link to Pure Earth or the practices at all, or stand on its own?"
- "Three words for how it should feel?" (Prompt if needed: Scandinavian calm, athletic recovery, luxury spa, outdoor rugged.)
- If time: "Any brands you admire, or ones you definitely don't want to look like?"

**Prototype and website (3 min)**
> "The way I work, you see a clickable prototype of the site before anything is built in Shopify. You review it, we refine it, then it's built. That also means we can get going on the name, brand and prototype while you close the supplier deal."

- ★ "A new, separate Shopify store, or inside the Pure Earth account?" (Recommend separate.)
- ★ "How many products at launch, and what options: sizes, heaters, add-ons?"
- "Who'll update the site after launch: you, the VA, or me?"

**Email (2 min)**
- ★ "When you say email, do you mean mailboxes on the new domain, the order emails customers get, marketing emails, or all three?"
- "Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, any preference?"

**Bridge at 0:50:** "Last thing before I play it back: budget and timing."

---

## 8. Budget, timing and decisions (5 min, 0:50 to 0:55)

- ★ "When would you like to be live, and is there a reason for that date?" (If January: "January is the peak month for this market. It's tight from now, but possible if we keep the first phase focused.")
- ★ "Do you have a budget in mind, for the build and for any ongoing help? A range is fine."
- "Are you talking to anyone else, or thinking of setting it up yourselves like Pure Earth?"
- "How do you like to work: calls, WhatsApp, a shared review link?"

If they ask for a price on the spot:
> "My prices are published, so nothing will surprise you, but I'd rather send you options properly than guess. You'll have it by [date]."

**Hard stop 0:55.**

---

## 9. Play it back and next steps (5 min, 0:55 to 1:00)

> "Let me check I've got it." Play back in four lines:
1. What the business is and who it's for.
2. Where the supplier deal stands, and the open points.
3. What they want from you: name, brand, prototype, website, email.
4. Timing and budget.

> "Did I miss anything important?"

Then set out the likely shape, without prices:
> "I'll probably suggest two phases. First, name, brand and a clickable prototype, which can start now while you finish with the supplier. Second, the Shopify build, email and launch, once the supplier terms are signed. I'll send a proposal with options by [date], plus the list of points to agree with the supplier."

- Agree the date the proposal lands, and who it goes to (both of them).
- Agree how they'll send anything outstanding (supplier terms when they have them).

**End on time.**
