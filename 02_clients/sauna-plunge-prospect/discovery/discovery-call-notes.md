## 3. Viability (illustrative, for the proposal)

The clients raised this themselves. A short, honest worked example belongs in the proposal.

Assumptions: £4,000 average order including VAT; margin 10% to 20%; VAT registered, so VAT is due on the margin; Shopify Grow card fees.

| | 10% margin | 20% margin |
|---|---|---|
| Gross margin per order | £400 | £800 |
| Less VAT on the margin (1/6) | £333 | £667 |
| Less card fee (1.7% + 25p) | £265 | £598 |
| At 5 orders a month | **about £1,325** | **about £3,000** |

That monthly figure has to cover advertising, Shopify, apps and email before any profit, and profit is then split 50/50 with Vanessa. Klarna fees (about 5%, roughly £200 on a £4,000 order) would remove most of the margin at 10%. Google Ads cost per sale for a £4,000 product is unknown and could take most of it.

Implication: the business is only viable at the top of the margin range, with low acquisition costs, or with the 10% uplift applied. Stage 0 fees must stay proportionate, and a large build should not be proposed until the supplier terms and the ad cost are known.
