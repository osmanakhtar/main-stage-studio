# Questions for the Supplier

**For:** Shak and Rajan
**From:** Os, Main Stage Studio
**Date:** 18 October 2026

Questions to put to Outdoor Living 365 before agreeing a partnership. Ideally get the answers in writing.

## Questions for the supplier

1. **Margin and payment:** which margin applies to which products, and how and when do you pay them for each order?
2. **Pricing:** confirm the rule (their standard price, or up to 10% above) and whether it changes when they run sales.
3. **Branding:** can the products carry your brand? Will deliveries, paperwork and installers show their name or yours?
4. **Your customers:** will they agree in writing not to market to customers whose details they receive from you?
5. **Other resellers:** is anyone else selling the range, and is there any exclusivity by area?
6. **Orders and tracking:** how do orders reach them (Shopify Collective, an app, email), and how do tracking and delivery dates come back?
7. **Stock:** several products are sold out until November. Who gets stock first when it's short, and what are realistic lead times for January?
8. **Delivery and installation:** UK coverage, who books the slot, their own team or subcontractors, and who makes the electrical connection? Is there a site survey?
9. **Warranty and returns:** their warranty is one year and returns are faults only. Your customers' legal rights are with you and can go further, so how will they support you if a customer cancels or claims?
10. **Product information:** electrical ratings, safety certification, and whether you can use their photos, specs and reviews (including in the prototype).

## For the two of you to talk through

- **The numbers:** at a 10% to 20% margin, after VAT and card fees, each £4,000 order leaves roughly £265 to £600 to cover advertising and running costs before any profit is shared. Worth agreeing a monthly ad budget you're comfortable with.
- **Liability:** as the seller, you're responsible to customers for faults and cancellations, even though the supplier delivers. Worth a solicitor's view on your terms, and checking insurance.
- **VAT:** you'd pass the threshold at around two orders a month, so plan to register from the start.
- **Phone cover:** who takes calls from UK buyers in the evenings.
- **The name:** names that work well in search tend to be descriptive, which can make them harder to protect as a trademark. Worth weighing when we shortlist.
