**Prepared for:** Shak and Rajan
**Prepared by:** Main Stage Studio

**Status:** Draft for discussion

---

## 1. Summary

- **The supplier is small, new to this range and personal.** Outdoor Living 365 is an established online retailer (since 2017), but its SaunaPlunge range only launched in October 2025. Its strengths are fast delivery, sensible prices and very personal service. Its limits are capacity, stock and a thin brand.
- **SaunaPlunge prices are competitive.** Barrel and cabin saunas sit in line with or below UK kit specialists, with faster delivery when in stock. The Peak Plunge ice bath is a mid-premium product priced well above the popular chiller bundles.
- **The supplier's terms are tight on returns and warranty.** As the seller, you would carry your customers' legal rights, which can go further than the supplier's terms. This needs to be settled in your agreement with them.
- **The market is growing, and still fragmented.** No one dominates. Most competitors are kit sellers with slow lead times or regional installers. There is room for a brand that combines good advice, national reach and a smooth buying experience.
- **The opportunity is service and trust, not price.** You'll sell at the same prices as the supplier, so you win by being the easier, more reassuring place to buy.

### 2.4 Strengths and weaknesses

**Strengths**
- **Competitive prices** with delivery included, and fast delivery when in stock.
- **Recognised components:** Harvia heaters on the traditional saunas, thermo-treated timber.
- **Simple, clear installation pricing.**
- **Personal service** that customers clearly value.
- **A focused range,** easy to understand.
- **Finance at checkout** (Klarna, PayPal Credit).

**Weaknesses and risks for a reseller**
- **Capacity.** A small team, one installation service based in Yorkshire, serving the whole of the UK.
- **Stock.** Two of the nine main products are sold out until November, and the 6-person barrel is "regularly sold out", just ahead of the January peak. You need to know who gets stock first when it's short: their own customers or resellers.
- **Short warranty.** One year, where some competitors offer two on timber.
- **Strict returns terms.** No change-of-mind returns once dispatched, a 15% restocking fee and non-refundable deposits. As the seller, your customers' rights to cancel online purchases and to claim for faulty goods are owed by you, and can go beyond the supplier's terms. The gap would be your cost unless your agreement covers it. Worth a solicitor's view before you set your own terms.
- **Thin information.** No electrical ratings, manufacture details or planning guidance, which are among the first questions buyers ask.
- **A young brand** with few reviews.
- **Name confusion.** A separate company, Outdoor Living Hot Tubs (outdoorlivinghottubs.co.uk), sells barrel and infrared saunas under a similar name.
- **They are also your competitor,** selling the same products at the same prices direct.

### 2.5 Questions for the supplier

1. Trade margin (indicated at 10% to 20%: which products sit where?), how and when you pay them, and the pricing rule (indicated: their standard price, or up to 10% above it).
2. Whether you can sell under your own brand, and use the SaunaPlunge name in your marketing.
3. Stock allocation when products are short, and lead times for pre-orders.
4. Installation coverage across the UK, capacity in January to March, and who makes the electrical connection.
5. Warranty: how claims from your customers are handled, and whether they'll match what you promise.
6. Returns and cancellations: how they'll handle a customer exercising their rights with you.
7. Electrical ratings, manufacture details and certification (UKCA or CE) for every product.
8. Whether they'll agree not to market to your customers.
9. How orders reach them and tracking comes back (including Shopify Collective).
10. Whether other resellers sell the same range, and in which areas.

## 3. The market

- **Size and growth.** Market research puts the UK sauna sector (commercial and home) at about £589 million in 2025 to 2026, with forecasts of around 7.3% to 7.5% growth a year to 2033. A separate UK cold plunge forecast is similar, at about 7.5% a year. These are estimates from research firms, repeated in trade press.
- **Culture shift.** The number of public saunas in the UK has grown from about 45 in 2023 to over 600, by the British Sauna Society's count (other sources cite up to 760). Public sauna use introduces people to the habit, and home ownership follows.
- **Seasonality.** Retailers report a spike in enquiries and sales in the first quarter of the year.
- **Typical spend.** UK guides put a ready-made outdoor sauna at roughly £2,000 to £15,000, and bespoke builds at £20,000 and above.

### 4.3 What the comparison shows

1. **SaunaPlunge saunas are fairly priced.** The 4-person barrel is within about 4% of Layzee Living's sale price for a similar size, with much faster delivery and installation on offer. The infrared range sits slightly above the budget infrared brands, with outdoor models a step up.
2. **The Peak Plunge needs explaining.** At £4,200 it is nearly three times the most visible chiller bundle. Buyers will compare it with Lumi first. Its case (a fixed tub, built-in filtration, always ready) has to be made clearly, or it won't sell.
3. **Speed is a real advantage, when stock is there.** Most kit specialists quote weeks; SaunaPlunge quotes about 10 days. Stock-outs ahead of January undermine it.
4. **Few competitors combine advice, installation and national reach.** Kit sellers leave buyers to work out bases, electrics and assembly. Premium and regional players do it well, but at higher prices or in limited areas.
5. **Finance is standard.** Most serious competitors offer it; it isn't a differentiator.
6. **Reviews are thin across the board.** Layzee Living's saunas have 0 to 5 reviews each; the supplier has 8 on Trustpilot. Building visible reviews early is achievable and will stand out.

## 5. Where a new brand can win

You'll sell the same products at the same prices as the supplier, so the business has to win on the buying experience.

| Gap in the market | How you could fill it |
|---|---|
| Buyers don't know what they need (base, electrics, space, traditional or infrared) | An advice-led site: buying guides, a simple product finder, a suitability check before purchase |
| Kit sellers leave installation to the buyer | A clear "delivered, built and working" offer, with installation and electrics arranged, priced upfront |
| Thin product information | Full specs, electrical requirements and honest answers on planning, running costs and maintenance |
| Few reviews, young brands | A review programme from the first order; real customer photos |
| Short warranties | Clear aftercare, and if the supplier agrees, a longer warranty than theirs |
| Generic, product-led brands | A distinctive, wellness-led brand with a clear point of view |
| Your own network | Introductions to people who already trust you, if you choose to use them |

**Positioning options to discuss**

- **The trusted advisor:** "We help you choose, check your garden, and hand it over working." Advice and service first.
- **The recovery specialist:** sauna and cold plunge as a pair, aimed at fitness and recovery buyers.
- **The calm luxury brand:** premium, design-led, aimed at homeowners creating a garden retreat.

These need choosing before naming and brand work, because they shape both.

## 6. Risks to plan for

| Risk | Why it matters | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Supplier capacity and stock | Small team, products already selling out before the January peak | Agree stock allocation and lead times in writing; show honest delivery dates on the site |
| Returns and warranty gap | You owe customers rights that can exceed the supplier's terms | Agree back-to-back terms; take a solicitor's view on your customer terms |
| Selling against the supplier | Same products, same prices, sold direct | Differentiate on service and brand; agree they won't market to your customers |
| Installation coverage | One installation service, based in Yorkshire | Confirm coverage, or line up regional installers and electricians |
| Peak Plunge price | Expensive against popular alternatives | Make its case clearly, or lead with saunas |
| Name confusion | Two similarly named sauna sellers exist | Make sure your own brand stands clearly apart |
