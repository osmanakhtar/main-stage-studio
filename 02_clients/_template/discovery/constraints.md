## 01 Spatial character

**What is this brand's spatial character?**
Dense or airy. Grid-rigid or organic. Static or kinetic. Decide before touching any code.

> 

**What layout conventions are explicitly off-limits?**
Name at least two. The act of naming them breaks your defaults.

## 02 Emotional register

**What does this brand feel like — not look like?**
Three words. Not visual descriptors. Emotional register: urgent, tender, irreverent, precise, raw, quiet, electric.

> 

**What references exist outside the web?**
Music, fashion, print, film, architecture. Breaks you out of web-pattern defaults before the first commit.

> 

---

## 03 What this brand is not

**Visual language to avoid**
Colours, type choices, texture, density that would be wrong for this client.

> 

**Emotional register to avoid**
The mood or tone that would be a wrong read on who this client is.

> 

**Component patterns to avoid**
Specific layout or UI patterns carried over from MSS or previous client work.

> 

**Comparators to avoid**
Agencies, studios, or competitors whose aesthetic this client actively doesn't want to echo.

>
