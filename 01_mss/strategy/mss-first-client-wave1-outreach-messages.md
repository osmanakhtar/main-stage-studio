# Wave 1 outreach messages

Drafted 16 August 2026, against the 24 firms in `mss-first-client-wave1-list.md`
(11 conveyancing, 13 accountancy). Revised twice on 17 August 2026 after copy
review.

**First revision** fixed three things: the original draft repeated the same
pitch sentence near-verbatim across most of the accountancy batch, opened
every message with deficit framing ("no booking", "no AML step") that read
as criticism rather than observation, and never named Main Stage Studio
anywhere in the body, leaving the sender unidentified until the reader
clicked the link.

**Second revision** reordered and reworded on two further points: (1) each
email now leads with what MSS builds (value) before the firm-specific
observation (pain), on the basis that the reader already knows their own
process and doesn't need it explained back to them at length before hearing
the offer; the observation is now a single compressed proof-point sentence
rather than an opening paragraph. (2) Voice now mixes "we" and "I"
deliberately, following the precedent in `mss-tone-of-voice.md`'s own
Proposal/Direct Client Communication example: "we" for what MSS builds and
delivers (the capability, the work), "I" for the personal observation, the
ask, and the sign-off. This positions MSS as more than one person without
losing the founder-to-founder register that's doing real work when the
recipient is themselves a partner or founder of a small practice.

**Process note (not reflected in the email copy, per Osman's decision):**
Kurshid will run business-development follow-up calls with prospects who
reply. This is an internal handoff after the first response, not something
the cold email itself should reference, introducing a second unfamiliar
name before there's even a reply adds friction rather than credibility.

Links updated 17 August 2026 to the hosted tenant prototypes: conveyancing
solicitors get `https://www.mainstagestudio.co.uk/work/booking-engine/prototype/?tenant=thackray`
(Thackray Vane), accountancy practices get
`https://www.mainstagestudio.co.uk/work/booking-engine/prototype/?tenant=marbury`
(Marbury Hale).

**Before sending:** two firms (Prescient Accounting, Halliday Styan) carry
open caveats in the source list (registered office/multi-region question) —
resolve those before sending to avoid a wrong "local" claim landing badly
with a compliance-minded reader. Six firms have no named contact; those
messages below use a firm-name-only greeting and should go to the general
inbox unless a name turns up first. Two firms had multiple named directors
(Passman Leonard, Halliday Styan); one has been picked per firm below to
address directly, per the plan's own note not to address several people at
once.

Cadence per the plan: send in batches of 5-8/day, not all 24 at once. One
follow-up after 5-7 days of silence on the same thread, then stop (Kurshid
picks up from there on any reply, per the process note above).
