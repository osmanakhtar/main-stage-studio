## Downgraded on closer look (contact-enrichment pass)

- **Meaby & Co Solicitors** (Clerkenwell) — was in the original 25-firm
  count with a caveat flagging fast recent growth. Confirmed this pass: the
  firm has offices in Clerkenwell, Camberwell, Chigwell, Loughton and
  Dorking, and its Property Department alone is headed by a Managing
  Partner (Chris Sarsfield) over a team of 14 lawyers. That's well past the
  2-8 partner target profile. Moved here, dropped from the working list.
- **YVA Solicitors** (North Finchley) — one of the two firms that 403'd
  automated fetch in passes 1 and 2. Resolved via LinkedIn this pass: the
  firm's own company page lists 51-200 employees, formed from an
  amalgamation of three North London practices, with at least three named
  partners (Jonathan Bishop, Paul Yiannakas, Shamil Patel). Too large for
  the target profile, was never added to the working list, no longer
  "unresolved," now excluded outright.
