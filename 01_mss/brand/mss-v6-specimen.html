01 · The mark

Spike.

Stage managers tape two corners on the floor to mark exactly where a performer stands. The mark is that tape. It frames empty space, because the thing that goes inside it is always the client's.

40 × 40 grid · stroke 5 · square caps · min 20px

A crop frame

The work is framed, never recoloured. Each client keeps their own palette inside it.

One Tungsten moment per view.

If two things are lit, nothing is. Prices, the primary button and the mark are the usual candidates.

Tungsten is never text on light.

It fails contrast on House Lights. On light pages it becomes a fill with black type on top, or the mark turns black.

Cue Red means live or wrong.

Dots, borders and fills for "live now" and errors. Never small text (only 4.6:1 on black, less on raised surfaces), never decoration, never a second accent.
