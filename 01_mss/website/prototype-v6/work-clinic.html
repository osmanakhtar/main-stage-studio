Demo, fictional client · Platform · Clinic

Consent that stands up in a file review.

A booking and consent journey for Vera Aesthetics, a fictional nurse-led clinic. Not a scheduler with a form bolted on: signatures that hold up as evidence, a screening step that can decline or reroute a booking, and a rule that will not let anyone book a prescription treatment without a consultation first.

Product

Platform

Sector

Medical aesthetics

Status

Working demo, open to try

The problem

A clinic handles health data, consent that has to stand up later, and medicines it is not allowed to name in public. A generic booking tool with a PDF consent form does not touch any of that.

The clinic needs to open the record for any booking and show exactly what was asked, what was signed, and when.

What I built

Consultation before treatment

Pick a prescription treatment with no consultation on record and the journey sends you to a consultation instead, with the reason on the page.

Signatures that stand up

A drawn or typed signature, each clause acknowledged and timestamped, and the signed document fingerprinted so it can be checked later.

Photo consent kept separate

Consent to treatment and consent to before-and-after photos are two signatures, because refusing one must never mean losing the other.

Medicines never named

Prescription medicines cannot be advertised, so the site, the journey and the confirmation use a public treatment name instead.

Where it stands

A working demo, not a mock-up. The rules fire against what you choose, and every booking ends with a record the clinic can open and show.
