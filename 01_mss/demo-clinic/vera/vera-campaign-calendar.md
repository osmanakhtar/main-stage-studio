## Per-post detail

Hooks are in Vera's voice; full captions drafted at production, lint-checked before Stage.

**1. "Will it make me look frozen?" (Education carousel)**
Myth-bust. Slides: the fear → what wrinkle-softening actually does → why amount and placement decide the result → "you should still look surprised." Compliance: no product named; framed as "wrinkle-softening treatment."

**2. "Inside the clinic" (Experience single)**
The room, calm and unhurried. Hook: "No rush, no pressure, no upsell at the door." Compliance: none.

**3. "Your skin, better rested" (Results single)**
Quiet skin-quality post, not a before/after. Hook: "Most people won't know what changed. They'll just think you look well." Compliance: mark *illustrative*; no outcome guarantees.

**4. "A prescriber who never says no..." (Authority quote card)**
Navy card, gold rule, Fraunces. The honesty positioning in one line. Compliance: none.

**5. "What healthy skin actually looks like" (Education carousel)**
Skin-health literacy: texture, hydration, why "glass skin" filters mislead. Uses macro skin stills. Compliance: no unrealistic claims.

**6. "What to expect on the day" (Experience single)**
Treatment walkthrough, device b-roll. Hook: "Nervous is normal. Here's exactly what happens." Compliance: device is nameable; no POM.

**7. "Skin-health season starts now" (Offers single)**
Seasonal, autumn prep. Soft CTA. Hook: "If it's right for your skin, we'll tell you. If it isn't, we'll tell you that too." Compliance: no POM naming; honest offer terms.

**8. "I look like me, just less tired" (Patient voice quote card)**
Illustrative testimonial treatment. Compliance: labelled illustrative for the demo; in a real clinic this needs genuine, consented wording.

**9. "3 questions to ask before any treatment" (Education carousel)**
Safety and choosing well: is the injector a prescriber, what happens if something goes wrong, will they say no. Compliance: authority-building, no claims.

**10. "The consultation is the product" (Experience single)**
The consult shot. Hook: "Your face isn't a menu." Compliance: none.

**11. "Skin that ages well" (Results single)**
Skin-longevity angle, jawline glow still. Hook: "The goal isn't younger. It's well-kept." Compliance: mark *illustrative*.

**12. "You should leave looking like you, on a good day" (Authority quote card)**
Closes the campaign on the brand line. Navy card, gold rule. Compliance: none.
