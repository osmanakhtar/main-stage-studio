# MSS brand book: the slate

*13 Oct 2026. Proposal, nothing designed yet. Os's call: a brand book of fictional brands MSS would build,
chosen for creative range rather than ICP fit, with one of them carried end to end through the hero film
("Source to stage", `01_mss/website/prototype-v6/hero-film-concepts.md`).*

---

## The rule that makes it honest

**The brands are fictional. The sources are real.** Every brand grows from one real-world thing you can
point at: a public tide table, a pool's tiling, a book's margins. The hero film opens each chapter on that
source, so the design thinking it shows is genuine even when the client isn't. Every spread and every
frame is badged **Self-initiated**, the same as Sable.

Also binding:
- No real business imitated: each name gets checked (Companies House, a web search, Instagram) before any
  design work, and each brand lives in a named fictional or unclaimed place.
- Each brand has its own type and colour, never Archivo, Geist Mono or Tungsten, so studio and client
  never blur (brand identity, Type rules).
- No generated people, no glossy AI imagery. Photography is shot by Os, licensed, or public domain.
- Every rejected exploration shown in the book or film is a real one we actually made.

## Each spread (same for every brand)

1. **The source** (the real thing, photographed or reproduced)
2. **What we took from it** (three observations, one line each)
3. **Explorations** (three real routes, side by side)
4. **The cut** (the route chosen, and one line on why)
5. **The system** (wordmark, palette, type, one layout, one application in use)

## The hero brand: Low Tide (recommended)

The film needs a brand that runs through all four chapters for real. Low Tide is the only one where every
chapter is natural, and chapter 4 comes out of the business itself:

| Chapter | Low Tide |
|---|---|
| Identity | Tide table, then numerals and timetable rules, three real routes, the cut to one wordmark |
| Website | The wordmark becomes the header; the home page leads with today's low tide and when the bar opens |
| Content | Catch-of-the-day photo tried at three crops; 4:5 wins and drops into the grid |
| Campaign and Platform | "Tide's out Saturday at 2:40. Table for two?": the booking email sent from the tide table itself, through to the booking. *Designed like a page. Built like a system.* |

Its weak point: Content needs real food and harbour photography. Os would need to shoot it (one coastal
trip, or a fishmonger's counter and a plate), or we license it. Not generated.

## Order of work

1. Os picks the slate (3 to 5) and confirms Low Tide as the hero brand.
2. Name and conflict checks for each pick, plus a fictional place for Low Tide.
3. Low Tide identity, made for real (source, three routes, the cut, the system). This doubles as the
   hero's chapter 1 material.
4. Hero chapter 1 pilot in the cue pipeline, judged on Stage against the current type-only hero.
5. The rest of Low Tide (site, content, journey), then the other brands' spreads, then the brand book
   page on the site (Identity proof plus a Work section).
