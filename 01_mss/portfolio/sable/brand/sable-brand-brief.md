# Sable — Brand Brief

*Client document. Built by Main Stage Studio, 31 May 2026.*
*Source of truth for all design and copy decisions on this engagement.*

---

## The Artist

Sable is a young Black British musician, born and raised in London, early twenties. She makes soulful, genre-defying music on her own terms. Her visual world is as distinctive as her sound: rocky, punky dress sense, gold jewellery, braids. She carries herself with quiet authority.

She is unapologetically herself. That's not a brand position. It's just who she is.

---

## The Creative Insight

Sable contains multitudes and she doesn't resolve the contradiction for you.

Soft and sharp. Soul and grit. Vulnerable in the music, guarded in the world. The brand doesn't flatten that tension. It lives in it.

---

## Positioning

Sable is not a genre. She's not a scene. She's not a movement.

She's an artist from London who makes music on her own terms. The copy never explains what the music sounds like. The site is not about the music. It's where the music lives.

---

## Identity

### Wordmark

`sable` in wide-tracked serif, lowercase throughout. Nothing is capitalised. Nothing is decorated. The name takes up the space it deserves and no more.

Font direction: high-contrast serif, weight 300 to 400. Wide letter-spacing, approximately 8 to 10 units. The spacing is the character.

### Descriptor

`london` sits beneath the wordmark. Lowercase, tracked wide, in gold. Small enough to be a whisper. Present enough to matter.

London is not decorative geography. For a young Black British artist making soulful music, it's cultural context. It earns its place.

### Logo system

The full mark is the wordmark plus the descriptor. Nothing else. No icon, no symbol, no supporting graphic. At this stage the name carries itself.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Role |
|------|-----|------|
| Bone | #F0E8D8 | Primary ground, homepage, arrival |
| Night | #0D0C0B | Deep ground, music sections, immersive content |
| Coal | #1C1A18 | Secondary dark surface |
| Ash | #3D3530 | Tertiary dark surface, texture within dark range |
| Gold | #C89442 | Accent, descriptor, punctuation throughout |

### Ground system

Bone leads. It's the first thing encountered. Intimate, unguarded, present.

Dark sections follow as the site deepens: music, atmosphere, the more immersive content.

Gold runs as the consistent thread throughout. Never dominant. Always purposeful.

### Colour principles

One accent colour in any composition. Never two.
Gold is punctuation, not wallpaper.
Bone is never replaced by stark white.
Night is never replaced by pure black.
The warmth in every colour is intentional. Nothing is cold.

---

## Typography

### Display and wordmark

High-contrast serif. Cormorant Garamond or equivalent. Weight 300 to 400 only.
Headlines: large, tracked tight or wide depending on context, always left-aligned.
No centred type anywhere on the site.

### Body and UI

Geometric humanist sans. Plus Jakarta Sans or equivalent. Weight 300 to 400.
Body: 16px, relaxed line-height, max-width 60ch.
Navigation and labels: small, wide-tracked, lowercase.

### Typography principles

Lowercase throughout, always. The wordmark sets the rule for the whole site.
No all-caps except navigation labels at small sizes with wide tracking.
No bold weight in body copy.
Type does the work. Nothing else needs to.

---

## Photography Direction

### Lead register: intimate and cinematic

Close. Warm. Low available light.
The kind of image that feels like it wasn't meant to be seen by many people.
Skin, texture, gold detail. Nothing posed, everything intentional.
She is present, not performing.

### Lighting language

Practical sources only: candles, a single lamp, daylight through a gap.
Never studio lit. Never clean. The light finds her.

### Colour temperature

Warm, amber-leaning. The gold in the palette is the quality of the light itself, not a separate decision.

### As the site deepens

Raw and urban: London at night, concrete, she is the still point.
Otherworldly: abstract, textured, atmospheric, not tied to any time or place.

### What is never in frame

Crowds, stages, spectacle, hype. The intimate version of Sable doesn't need an audience in the shot.

### Production

All photography AI-generated via Higgsfield. Built to narrative, not constrained by existing assets. Each shoot brief derived from this document.

## Web Presence

### Pages

Three sections, single page. No more at this stage.

Homepage: the arrival. Wordmark, descriptor, one line of copy, one CTA.
Music: where the work lives. Track or EP listing, no description of sound.
Connect: one line, one input, nothing else.

### Layout principles

Asymmetric, editorial. Never centred.
Space does the work. Generous margins, sparse content.
No generic card layouts. No feature grids. No carousels.
Motion used as punctuation: one moment per section, not decoration throughout.

### Code principles

Bone as default background.
Hardware-accelerated motion only: transform and opacity.
Tailwind CSS.
No emoji. No gradient backgrounds. No purple or blue anywhere.

### CTA language

Always a single word.
listen. write. nothing more.

---

## What This Brand Is Not

Not a music industry press release.
Not a genre exercise.
Not a beauty brand despite the warmth of the palette.
Not eager. It doesn't reach for the audience. The audience comes to it.
Not explained. Never "Sable is a London-based artist who..."
Not MSS. The aesthetic languages of this brand and the studio are completely separate.
