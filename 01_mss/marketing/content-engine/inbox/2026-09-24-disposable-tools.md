Captured by Claude from Osman's brief on 24 Sep 2026 ("the value of building disposable tools to achieve outcomes, and the services I provide"). Osman to confirm the thesis is his before any draft is approved.

- Cheap, single-purpose tools are now an afternoon, not a week. Build them to answer one question, then delete them.
- The outcome is what's kept: a decision made on evidence, a site that shipped right, a process that holds.
- Real examples in the studio's own record:
  - A recent client build: ten standalone prototypes explored and dropped. The go-live site is two pages (Home, plus one Treatments page with eleven anchored sections), signed off by the client in Stage. Client not named in posts.
  - The homepage onboarding demo clip is produced by a script driving the published prototype. Captions are read from the live rule trace; the run fails if a caption names a rule that never fired. The clip is regenerated whenever the prototype changes.
- Boundary: scaffolding is disposable, anything the client's client touches is not. Systems work stays deterministic and config-driven (v5 §5.2).
