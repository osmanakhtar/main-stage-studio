# Case study / proof

**Job:** convert credibility into evidence. Turn a piece of real client work into a decision-by-decision read, not a portfolio blurb.

**Typical formats:** a before/after, a specific decision explained (why this positioning, why this structure), a metric with the story behind it.

**Source:** an inbox item describing a real, signed-off piece of client work. Confidentiality matters more here than in any other pillar; only draft from work the client has actually cleared for publication.

## Voice

Company voice: "we." Lead with the client's world and problem before the studio's process, per the case-study-opener pattern in `mss-tone-of-voice.md` §Voice in Practice: the problem emerges from the person, not from a brief.

## Draft instructions

1. Read the inbox item. Identify the actual decision or tension it describes, not just the outcome.
2. Open with the client's situation, in their terms, not the studio's offer.
3. Name the specific decision the studio made and why, concretely enough that a reader could disagree with it if it were wrong.
4. If there's a result or metric, state it plainly, no adjectives doing work the number should do.
5. Target length: 100-180 words.

## Guardrails

- Never draft from an inbox item unless it's marked cleared for publication (or Osman confirms it verbally when asked to draft).
- No invented metrics or outcomes. If the inbox item doesn't have a number, don't add one.
- Don't overstate MSS's role if the work was collaborative; be precise about what the studio actually did.
