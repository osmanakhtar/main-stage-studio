Before we scope an onboarding system, we build one we fully intend to throw away.

It's a working prototype: real questions, real branching, real rules deciding who sees what next. It runs in a browser, and it's cheap enough to discard. There's one on our site you can click through now.

Its job is to surface what a requirements document hides. Which answers send a client to manual review? What happens when two rules disagree? Where does someone give up?

Once those are answered on screen, in front of the people who run the process, the real build starts from a specification, not a guess.

If your onboarding still runs on email, PDFs and memory, that's where the conversation starts.
