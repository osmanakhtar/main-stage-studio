The onboarding demo on our site isn't recorded by hand. A script records it.

It drives the live prototype, steps through each decision, and captions every step with the rule that fired. The captions aren't typed. They're read out of the engine's own rule trace. Ask for a caption on a rule the engine never applied, and the recording fails.

We built it because the first clip went stale the moment the prototype moved. Re-recording by hand meant re-checking every caption by hand, and that's exactly where a wrong claim slips through.

So the clip is disposable. We delete it and regenerate it whenever the product changes. What we kept is the guarantee: the demo can't say something the system doesn't do.
