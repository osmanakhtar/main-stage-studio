Disposable has a boundary. In regulated work, where you draw it is the whole job.

The scaffolding can go: prototypes, test harnesses, one-off checks, the mock journey that shows a firm where its clients stall. Build it fast, learn from it, delete it.

What a client touches can't. The live onboarding journey. The rule that decides who sees which disclosure. The intake form that feeds an identity check. Those get built once, deliberately, from configuration rather than one-off code, and they change through review, never in a hurry.

The speed of the first is what pays for the care in the second. A question answered cheaply in week one is a mistake that never reaches a client in month six.

Fast where it's safe to be fast. Slow where it isn't.
