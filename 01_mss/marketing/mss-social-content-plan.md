# MSS Social Content Plan — LinkedIn + Instagram

*Created: 6 August 2026. Updated: 18 August 2026.*
*Status: page pipeline (content-engine, 4 of 5 pillars) live for v1, manual posting; personal-profile stream and Instagram still not run. See §8.*
*Companion docs: `mss-tone-of-voice.md`, `mss-decisions-log.md` (LinkedIn company page entry), `mss-clinic-content-model.md` (pillar/cadence pattern this borrows from).*

**Purpose:** MSS has no social presence beyond a claimed, empty LinkedIn page. The homepage is close to locked. This is the next build: a content engine for the studio itself, using the same review-gated pipeline already proven on FSC and PureMed, pointed at MSS.

---

## 1. The positioning decision this plan is built on

Discussed and agreed 6 Aug 2026:

- **No headshots, no personal disclosure** stays as a website rule (`mss-website-brief.md`), and it does **not** block a founder point of view on social. Tone-of-voice already draws this line: *"The thinking is public. The person stays private."* Content carries Osman's judgment and opinions; it doesn't need his face.
- **LinkedIn is the conversion channel.** Regulated-business owners make hiring decisions there, and for a solo consultancy, founder point-of-view content (teardowns, opinions, process transparency) outperforms faceless company-page content. This is where the studio's own credibility narrative (product management background, "industries where a mistake is a breach") gets used, not just stated.
- **Instagram is the portfolio/awareness layer**, not the primary lead source for this audience. It carries case study visuals, process clips, and brand motion, content Higgsfield is well suited to generate. It supplements LinkedIn; it doesn't carry the sales case alone.

If Osman's comfort with visibility changes later (voice, screen presence, etc.), revisit this. Nothing below assumes an on-camera talking head.

## 3. Pillars

Five pillars. Each tagged with source and which platform it leads on.

| Pillar | Job | Typical formats | Source | Leads on |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Teardown / POV** | Prove judgment, generate the "who is this person" reaction | "Here's what's wrong with how [sector] sites talk about X", compliance-claim breakdowns, before/after critique of public examples | AI-drafted from Osman's finding, Osman edits + approves | LinkedIn |
| **2. Process transparency** | Prove the system is real, not a claim | Review-gate walkthroughs, pipeline clips, "here's what ships without a human reading it first" | AI-drafted from screen-capture + a one-line brief, Osman approves | Both |
| **3. Case study / proof** | Convert credibility into evidence | PureMed breakdown, before/after site metrics, decision-by-decision writeups | AI-drafted (existing case-study-to-LinkedIn-post workflow), Osman signs off | LinkedIn primary, IG cutdown |
| **4. Studio philosophy** | Carry the "thinking public, person private" voice | Product-thinking essays, "why regulated businesses get bad agencies", the discipline behind the work | AI-drafted from Osman's thesis, Osman edits + approves | LinkedIn |
| **5. Brand / visual** | Keep a feed worth following; portfolio texture | Abstract 3D-mark motion, palette studies, quote cards from pillar 1/4 posts | Fully AI-generated (Higgsfield), Osman selects | Instagram |

**Where AI stops:** the judgment. The teardown finding, the philosophy's thesis, the case study's honest read of what worked, that has to originate from Osman; AI can't invent a take. Everything downstream of the take (structuring, first-draft prose, tightening to voice) is AI-assisted, same review-gate pattern the client pipeline already uses: generate, then a human approves before it ships. The earlier "Osman, written" framing overstated how much of this needs to be typed by hand, it conflated authorship of the judgment with authorship of the draft.

**Deliberately excluded for now:** founder lifestyle content, on-camera talking-head video, personal disclosure of any kind. Matches the standing site rule; revisit only if Osman explicitly wants to change it.

## 4. The monthly mix

Realistic cadence for a solo operator who is also running client delivery: **not** the clinic model's 16/month. Target **8 LinkedIn posts + 8 Instagram posts per month** (roughly 2/week each), reviewed after one month and adjusted.

| Pillar | LinkedIn / mo | Instagram / mo | Source |
|---|---|---|---|
| Teardown / POV | 3 | 1 (quote-card cutdown) | Osman |
| Process transparency | 2 | 2 | Screen capture + Higgsfield |
| Case study / proof | 1 | 2 | Client work |
| Studio philosophy | 2 | 0 | Osman |
| Brand / visual | 0 | 3 | Higgsfield |
| **Total** | **8** | **8** | — |

Read: LinkedIn is 5/8 Osman-authored point-of-view, the actual conversion lever. Instagram is 5/8 Higgsfield/visual, the layer that needs credits to exist at all.

## 5. What Higgsfield actually needs to produce

Scoped against the pillars above, not generic "brand content":

- **Brand/visual (IG, ~3/mo):** short motion loops and stills built from the existing 3D mark and MSS palette (#F5EFE5 / #BF6B47 / #8C4A2F / #1C1712). Same asset family already scoped for the homepage's Gap and service-card sections, one generation pass can likely serve both.
- **Process transparency b-roll (both, ~2/mo):** supplementary motion to sit behind or around real screen-capture footage of the review gate / pipeline, not a replacement for it. The real tool footage is the credibility signal; Higgsfield dresses it.
- **Quote-card templates:** static, reusable frame for pulling a line out of a LinkedIn POV post and reposting it to IG. One template built once, reused monthly.

This is a small, bounded ask, not open-ended generation. Estimate before buying credits: a template/pattern batch (quote card + 2-3 motion loop variants) up front, then light top-ups monthly as new posts are cut down.

## 6. Weekly rhythm (proposed)

No content-day equivalent exists here (there's no clinic to visit), Osman *is* the capture source for the pillar that matters most. Rhythm instead of a batch day:

- **Writing block (weekly, ~30-45min):** Osman supplies the take, findings, or thesis for 2 LinkedIn posts (bullet points or a voice note), AI drafts, Osman edits and approves. The bottleneck is the judgment, not the typing, so this should be materially shorter than a from-scratch writing session.
- **Cutdown pass (weekly, ~30min):** pull one line from that week's LinkedIn post into a quote-card for IG.
- **Pipeline/case-study capture:** opportunistic, whenever real client or tool work produces something worth showing (a shipped feature, a caught compliance issue, a before/after). Logged as it happens, not scheduled.
- **Higgsfield batch:** monthly, not per-post, generate a month's worth of brand/visual assets in one pass.
- **Review gate:** same Stage-based approval pattern as client work. Osman approves before anything posts. No new tooling needed, the studio already has this.

## 7. Compliance note

MSS itself is not a regulated business, so the compliance load here is much lighter than client content: no POM rules, no ASA before/after standards. The main constraints are ordinary ones: no overclaiming results on Osman's own behalf, client confidentiality respected until a case study is signed off for publication, and no personal disclosure per the standing site rule.
