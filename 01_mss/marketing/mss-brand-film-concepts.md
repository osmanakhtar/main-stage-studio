# MSS Brand Films: Concepts

*Created: 6 August 2026. Run through the creative-director method (intake, insight, ideation, evaluation, articulation).*
*Status: PROPOSAL. Nothing generated yet. Companion to `mss-social-content-plan.md` (Instagram is the visual/motion layer these films feed) and `mss-positioning-v5-regulated-services.md` (locked positioning these films argue from).*
*Production: Higgsfield MCP, image-to-video. Production rules in §7 are binding, not suggestions.*

---

## 1. Brief

Six short brand films promoting Main Stage Studio to small and boutique regulated or trust-sensitive professional services firms in the UK (IFAs, boutique wealth managers, insurance brokers, accountants, legal practices, regulated fintechs), per v5 §3. Buyer is the principal or partner, the person whose name is on the firm and who personally signs off anything published. Objective is to land the first regulated-services client. Channels: LinkedIn primary (sound-off, feed), Instagram secondary. Constraints: no employer ever named, no founder face, no client faces, no invented stats, no client counts, brand palette and typography locked, everything produced through Higgsfield by one person.

**Idea level required:** `campaign` (a platform with executions), not six unconnected `execution`-level films. See §3 for why that distinction changed the answer.

---

## 2. Insight

Work through the category first. Every marketing agency sells the same three things: more output, more reach, more boldness. For this buyer, all three are descriptions of risk. That is the category tension, and it is unusually wide here.

Then the human one. Ask why a firm with genuinely good work has a website last touched in 2019. The convenient answer is "too busy." It is wrong. In a regulated firm the downside is asymmetric: a good post gets you one enquiry, a bad post is an incident with the principal's name on it. Doing nothing is not a failure of will. It is a correct reading of the risk.

**Insight:**

> The principal of a small regulated firm wants the firm to be as visible as it is good, but does nothing about it, because in their world a bad piece of marketing is not a wasted spend, it is a breach with their name on it, and silence is the only option that has never got anyone in trouble.

Passes the quality test on all six checks. It is recognisable, it is not a category platitude, it contains an unresolved tension, and it produces a directive.

**Advantage:** MSS is the only studio in this buyer's consideration set where the person judging the risk has spent a career on the risk side, and where the approval gate is mechanically enforced rather than promised.

**Strategy, one sentence:**

> Do not sell the output. Sell the gate. Make the studio's most attractive feature the fact that it stops things.

That is a genuine inversion of the category. It is also literally true of how MSS works, which is what stops it being a positioning trick.

## 3. Where I disagree with the brief

**Six varied commercials is the wrong unit.** Variety without a spine produces six orphans that each start from zero. What follows is one platform with six films inside it, deliberately split across three registers (two real-world metaphor, two abstract, two direct confrontation) so the variety is real without the set fragmenting. The register mix is also a hedge: if the metaphor films read as too arty for an IFA principal, the direct films carry the load, and we will know within two posts which is true.

**Films alone will not make the phone ring.** The addressable universe here is a few thousand UK firms, and their principals do not watch commercials. The films are the brand layer and they are worth making. The thing that actually generates enquiries is in §6, Extra A, and it is not a film.

**The credential and the privacy rule are in conflict, and no film can dodge it.** v5 bets the whole position on the founder's track record. The brand rule says the person stays private. Extra B resolves this by making the withholding the idea itself. I think it deserves to be film seven rather than an extra.

**Campaign line (sits under the locked hero, does not replace it):**

> Careful, in public.

The hero line ("You don't need a bigger team. You need a better system.") stays exactly as locked. "Careful, in public" is the campaign-level compression of the insight: careful and visible are supposed to be opposites, and this studio makes them one decision.

## 4. The six films

Scores are weighted per the six criteria (originality .25, strategic fit .20, emotional response .20, feasibility .15, scalability .10, simplicity .10), then HumanKind and Grey.

---

### Film 1: THE LOCK
*Register: real-world metaphoric symbolism. Role: platform film. Pattern P17 / P07.*

**Concept:** A canal lock raises a boat by holding water back. The gate is not the delay, it is the mechanism.

**Visualisation** (9:16, approx 20s, four shots):

1. *(4s)* Macro, low sun. Black water inside a stone lock, still. Wet stone, the waterline stain, a brass ring bolt gone green. Nothing moves but light.
2. *(5s)* The timber gates close. Heavy, deliberate. Water squeezes black between them, then one seam of light.
3. *(5s)* The waterline climbs the stone. We see only the stone and the rising line, never the boat. Terracotta lichen passes out of frame at the top.
4. *(4s)* First wide shot of the film: the far gate opens onto open water and low gold light. Hold.
5. *End frame (post):* the two gate timbers meeting resolve into the MSS mark, two offset squares with the parchment cutout of light between them.

**Type (post, Cormorant Garamond):** "The gate is not what slows you down." / "It's what lifts you." / Main Stage Studio.

**Why it is not the obvious ad:** every agency film promises acceleration. This one promises a hold and argues the hold is the mechanism. The end frame is the reason to make this one first: the MSS mark is already two shapes meeting with light between them, which is a gate. No competitor can borrow that ending.

**Canon check:** closest adjacency is Guinness "Surfer" (P07, waiting as the reward). Difference in one sentence: Surfer made waiting a mythic ordeal to be endured, this makes the constraint mechanical and productive, aimed at a buyer whose caution is currently coded as a defect.

**Score:** 8.7 weighted / HumanKind 7.5 / Grey 8. Strong on originality, strategy and simplicity. Held under 9 by the same thing holding the whole set under 9, see §9.

### Film 2: THE HALLMARK
*Register: real-world metaphoric symbolism. Role: what sign-off actually is. Pattern P17, SIT Task Unification.*

**Concept:** Silver is worth nothing until the assay office cuts it, tests it, and strikes a mark into it. The mark is what makes it worth something.

**Visualisation** (9:16, approx 18s):

1. *(4s)* Macro: raw silver, unmarked, warm light against near-black. Beautiful, and worth nothing yet.
2. *(4s)* A hand (no face, ever) sets a punch against it. Slow. The punch is the only sharp object in frame.
3. *(3s)* The strike. One frame of impact, speed-ramped. The mark bites in, tiny and permanent.
4. *(4s)* Rack focus along the struck hallmark, then the metal turns and catches parchment-warm light.
5. *(3s)* Match cut on the same downward motion: a cursor pressing Approve in a plain review interface, near-black on parchment.

**Type:** "In some trades, nothing is worth anything until someone puts their mark on it." / "Yours goes on everything here."

**Note:** the review interface in shot 5 is built in post to match Stage as it actually is. Do not generate invented software.

**Score:** 8.2 / HumanKind 7 / Grey 8. Feasibility is the highest in the set (macro metal, hands, fire and light are what AI video is genuinely good at).

---

### Film 3: ONE WORD
*Register: abstract, typographic. Role: why a generalist agency is dangerous. Pattern P13 Truth Telling.*

**Concept:** Remove one word from a sentence. It still reads perfectly. It is now a breach.

**Visualisation** (4:5 for LinkedIn, approx 15s):

1. Parchment under raking light. One line of type sits in frame. Plate generated, type set in post.
2. The final three words lift off the page and drift out of frame. The sentence closes the gap behind them and still scans perfectly.
3. Hold on the shortened sentence. It looks completely normal.
4. **Type:** "That sentence is now a financial promotion breach." / "Your agency did not see it leave."
5. **End:** "Main Stage Studio. Careful, in public."

**Why it is strong:** it is legible only to the exact buyer. Everyone else watches a sentence get shorter. That is a targeting mechanism disguised as a film, and it is the most efficient thing in the set for a tiny addressable audience.

**Series potential:** one per regime. An accountant's dropped caveat. A solicitor's outcome claim. A broker's implied cover. Same film, new sentence, indefinitely.

**Hard caution:** the specific examples must be verified before publishing. Getting a compliance example wrong inside an ad about compliance is the single worst failure available to this studio. Do not ship this film on a plausible-sounding sentence.

**Score:** 8.5 / HumanKind 7 / Grey 8. Originality 9 (nothing in the canon library is aimed this precisely). Feasibility marked down to 7 purely because the type has to be handled outside Higgsfield.

---

### Film 4: CHECKED FIVE TIMES
*Register: abstract. Role: the inversion, stated cleanly. SIT Attribute Dependency.*

**Concept:** Normally, the more something is checked the slower it goes. Invert the dependency: every check is what propels it further.

**Visualisation** (9:16, approx 15s):

A single line of type on parchment. Each time a mark lands on it (a check, a stamp impression, a small terracotta tick) the line leaps further out into the world: across the page, onto a screen, into a scrolling feed, onto a phone in a hand, onto a printed page on a wall. Mark, leap. Mark, leap. Five marks, five leaps. It ends a long way from where it started.

**Type:** "Checked five times." / "Which is why it got this far."

**Note:** this is closer to designed motion than generated footage. Generate the surfaces only (paper, screen glow, hand, wall) and do the travel in post, or use start-frame and end-frame control per leap.

**Score:** 8.2 / HumanKind 7 / Grey 7.5. The cleanest statement of the strategy in the set. Marked down on emotional response: it is clever before it is felt.

---

### Film 5: THE SAFEST THING YOU DID ALL YEAR
*Register: direct confrontation. Role: name the real competitor. Pattern P02, enemy = inertia.*

**Concept:** The real competitor is not another studio. It is doing nothing, and doing nothing has an excellent safety record.

**Visualisation** (9:16, approx 20s):

1. The firm's craft, shown honestly and without a single face. A hand annotating a client file in the margin, careful handwriting. A desk lamp at dusk. Two coffee cups, one long gone cold, after a meeting that went well. A door closing on an empty meeting room.
2. Cut to a laptop on the same desk. The firm's own website. Static. The copyright line reads 2019.
3. Hold. Longer than is comfortable. Nothing happens. That is the shot.
4. **Type:** "Nothing you published this year broke a single rule." / beat / "Nothing you published this year."
5. **Then:** "Silence is the only option that has never got anyone in trouble. It has also never got anyone a client."

**Why it earns its place:** this is the uncomfortable one, and every set needs one. It is also the film that does the arguing, so the metaphor films are free to do the feeling.

**Score:** 8.2 / HumanKind 7.5 / Grey 7.5. Highest strategic fit in the set (9.5): it attacks the actual thing standing between MSS and the sale.

---

### Film 6: NOBODY IS AT THE GATE
*Register: direct confrontation. Role: the AI honesty film. Pattern P02 / P06.*

**Concept:** Everyone now has a machine that never stops. The valuable part is the person who can stop it.

**Visualisation** (9:16, approx 18s):

1. A dark room. One screen, parchment-white light. An empty chair.
2. The screen produces posts. Handsome, plausible, and wrong in ways only a professional catches (a guarantee, an outcome promise, a missing risk warning). One is held on screen just long enough to read.
3. The rate accelerates past absurdity. Still nobody in the room.
4. A hand enters frame. One key. Everything stops. Silence.
5. **Type:** "Everyone has a machine that never stops now." / "The valuable part is the person who can." / "The system does the labour. You keep the moment."

**Why it matters:** it is the only film addressing what this buyer is currently being warned about in their own trade press, and it argues against the promise of the studio's own tooling. That is credibility bought at cost, which is the only kind that counts.

**Constraint:** the generated posts must be visibly generic. They must not resemble any identifiable real firm.

**Score:** 8.1 / HumanKind 7.5 / Grey 8. Emotion is Tier 3 ironic sincerity: a studio built on AI arguing that AI is not the point.

## 6. Extras, under my own direction

### Extra A: THE PLAIN ENGLISH REGISTER
*Level: `non_advertising`. This is the strongest idea in the document and it is not a film.*

A free, public, plain-English register of what a small regulated firm is and is not allowed to say in its own marketing, organised by regime (FCA financial promotions, ICAEW, SRA, and so on). Each entry: what the rule actually says, what it means for a post or a page, one compliant example, one non-compliant example, and the citation.

Built config-driven and deterministic, generated from a data file, exactly the way the wealth-onboarding use-case register is built. Same build pattern, different subject, and a build the founder can do faster than anyone else who would want to.

**Why it beats all six films on the objective:**
- It converts the credential from a claim into a usable object. Nobody has to believe a film.
- It is the Systems line demonstrated in public, and Systems currently has no public proof asset at all (v5 §5.2 says it launches on credential alone).
- It is searchable, linkable and permanent. The films decay in a feed within a week.
- Every film can end on it instead of on "start a project," which is a far lower-friction ask for someone who is not ready to buy.

**Activation test:** remove the campaign, does it still mean something? Yes. It is an act, not an execution.

**Hard caution:** it navigates what the rules say, with citations. It never gives advice. The disclaimer wording and the scope-of-responsibility line flagged in v5 §8 risk 5 both need to exist before this goes public.

### Extra B: THE ROOMS
*The credential film. Recommend promoting this to film seven rather than leaving it as an extra.*

v5 bets everything on the founder's track record. The brand rule says the person stays private. Rather than work around that, make the withholding the idea.

Twenty seconds of rooms, no faces, ever. A night operations floor. A wall of screens with a channel launching. A whiteboard with a customer journey mapped, wiped and re-mapped. A laptop open at 11pm on a half-built identity verification flow. Every shot holds one beat too long on the empty chair.

**Type:** "Twenty years in rooms that were not allowed to fail." / "You will not find a photograph of the person who was in them. You will find the work."

No employer is nameable from any frame. Nothing proprietary is shown. The constraint becomes the device.

**Score:** 8.05 / HumanKind 7.5 / Grey 7.5. It scored just outside the six, but it is the only film carrying the actual v5 bet, so it should be made.

## 7. Production rules (binding)

1. **No synthetic people presented as clients, colleagues, or the founder.** Hands only, or empty rooms. A studio selling trust cannot use fabricated humans as social proof. This is the single largest brand risk in producing any of this with AI video, and it is why every concept above is built without depicted people.
2. **All legible type is set in post**, in Cormorant Garamond and Plus Jakarta Sans. Never generate type in Higgsfield: it will be misspelled and off-brand, and in films 3 and 4 the type is the idea.
3. **Lock brand colour at the still stage.** Generate a start frame first with the palette named explicitly (parchment `#F5EFE5`, blush `#E8C9AE`, terracotta `#BF6B47`, ember `#8C4A2F`, near-black `#1C1712`), then image-to-video. Text-to-video drifts to a generic teal and orange grade every time.
4. **Aesthetic bans hold:** no purple or blue AI look, no gradient backgrounds, no centred compositions, no lens flare. Editorial stillness, one moving element per shot.
5. **Logo meaning is fixed** (corrected 6 Aug 2026): near-black is MSS's own discipline and infrastructure, terracotta is the client being carried and made visible. Any imagery reasoning from "black equals the vision" predates that correction and is wrong.
6. **Honesty constraints carry from v5:** no client counts, no auto-publish claim, no employer named, no invented figures, nothing implied by era that identifies a role.
7. **Generate silent.** Set sound off, score in post. LinkedIn plays sound-off first and every film above must read with no audio at all.

### Higgsfield model routing

| Need | Model | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Metaphor films (1, 2, 5, Extra B) | **Kling v3.0** | start_image plus end_image control, 3-15s, 9:16, multi-shot. Frame control is what keeps a 20 second edit on-brand. |
| Consistency across shots (lock stone, the silver) | **Seedance 2.0** | reference-driven, holds identity across clips, 4K available |
| The strike in film 2 | **Cinema Studio Video v2** | genre and speedramp control for the impact frame |
| Single reliable clip, no end-frame needed | **Veo 3** | reliable 9:16, fewer knobs |
| Derive 1:1 and 16:9 from the 9:16 master | **reframe** | avoids regenerating |

Aspect: 9:16 is the master for all films. Balance at time of writing: 1,174.5 credits, Plus plan.

### Production learnings, from actually shooting films 1 and 5 (7 Aug 2026)

These are proven on this pipeline, not predicted. Apply them to films 2, 3, 4, 6 and Extra B.

1. **Text-prompted mechanical action in image-to-video is unreliable. Force it with an end frame.** "The gates close" produced gates that stayed put while the camera panned. Re-running the same shot with a generated end frame (gates shut) in the `end_image` role delivered the close exactly. Any shot whose whole point is a specific change of state (something closing, opening, filling, rising, being struck) must be shot start-frame to end-frame, not prompted.
2. **Kling adds a slow camera drift no matter how firmly you specify locked-off.** Six separate "no pan, no drift, no zoom" instructions still produced slow push-ins. It reads as editorial rather than wrong, and on the empty-office shot it actively helps the uncomfortable hold. Budget for it: either accept it as the shot's motion, or stabilise in post. Do not keep re-rolling to get a true lock.
3. **Seedream's `image_references` role overrides the requested change.** Two attempts to generate "same wall, water risen higher" returned near-identical copies of the reference. The role preserves the scene at the expense of the instruction. For a changed-state end frame, expect this: it worked for the gates (a shape change) and failed twice for water level (a quantity change).
4. **When a beat will not generate, change the shot, not the prompt.** The rising waterline failed three ways (prompt, reference end frame, post reframe: the post cheat read as a camera tilt because everything moved at once). Replacing it with the lock chamber filling, water boiling up with foam, delivered the same meaning first time. Turbulence is texture and generates reliably; a measured level change is a quantity and does not.
5. **A defect check on the stills is not optional.** Of the first eight frames, three failed on brand grounds that no error message would ever surface: a sun flare and golden-hour drift, a blue dusk window, and generated browser chrome where a blank plate was needed. A fourth passed on composition but carried cyan and blue file tabs. All four were caught only by looking.
6. **Reserve pro mode for shots with real physics.** Water shots were run in pro, near-static interiors in std. The quality difference does not show on a still interior and pro is materially slower on turbulence.
7. **Preset interception.** Video submissions get intercepted by a recommended preset ("IN THE DARK") and silently fail rather than submit. Pass `declined_preset_id` to push past it, otherwise the stylised preset overrides the restrained direction.

## 8. Discarded directions

- **Stale website before and after.** The category's most common ad. Fails the specificity test instantly: swap in any competitor's logo and it still works.
- **The empty desk, the marketing hire you cannot afford.** Same problem, and worse, it anchors the price conversation at "cheaper than a person" instead of at value.
- **Founder to camera, or UGC talking head.** Breaks the person-stays-private rule, and would mean an AI-generated synthetic person representing a real founder, which is precisely the trust failure MSS positions against.
- **Long-form drama.** 261 canonical cases in the pattern library, extremely saturated, expensive to do well, and wrong for a sound-off feed.
- **Absurdist B2B comedy** (the EDS "Cat Herders" lineage). Genuinely tempting and proven for B2B, but 239 cases in the pattern and, more importantly, the wrong register: you cannot make a joke at the expense of a buyer whose whole anxiety is being taken seriously.

## 10. Recommendation

**Lead film:** The Lock. **Lead act:** The Plain English Register. Run them together, with the films pointing at the register.

**Make two first, not six.** Film 1 (The Lock) and Film 5 (The Safest Thing) are the two ends of the register range. Produce both, post both, see which gets replies. Producing all six before knowing whether the metaphor register lands with this buyer is spending credits on an untested assumption, and the assumption is genuinely uncertain.
