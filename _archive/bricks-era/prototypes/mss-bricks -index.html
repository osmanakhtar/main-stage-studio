02 — Process

How we build it

01

Discovery

We figure out what you're actually about before we make anything. Not a template, a genuine investigation into what makes this person and their work distinct. We start with questions, not assumptions.
02

Brand Expression

How you look, sound, and feel to the people you want to reach, built from the positioning outward, not assembled from trend boards. Every decision traceable back to what we found in discovery.
03

Design System

Everything needed to stay consistent across all surfaces: logo files, colour tokens, type specs, component rules. Built once so future work is faster and stays on-brand without the effort.
04

Web Presence

Designed as an HTML prototype first, then built in WordPress. Fast, client-owned, and straightforward to update. You don't need to come back to us every time something changes.
03 — Studio

You have something worth saying.
We find the form.

Main Stage was built by someone who spent years being effective at things that weren't quite the right fit. The rigour stayed. The expression got parked. This is what happens when you stop parking it.
The background is product management, which means one thing above everything else: understand the problem properly before you touch the solution. That discipline runs underneath everything the studio makes.
The tools have changed what’s possible. The judgment about what to do with them hasn’t changed hands.
Drawn to people whose work means something beyond the commercial. If what you are building matters to you, it matters here.
